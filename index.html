The Story

From pixels
to processes.

I didn't start in operations. I started with a code editor and a blank screen. Over nearly three years at JamesCoders, I designed and built 200+ responsive websites — HTML, CSS, SCSS, JavaScript — obsessing over the details that most people skip.

Then came the realization that the best product means nothing if the system behind it doesn't hold. So I moved into ops. And it turns out that building systems for businesses isn't so different from building interfaces — you're still thinking in structures, dependencies, and failure states.

Today I lead operations at The Implant Engine, a US-based dental marketing agency. I manage 150+ active client accounts, a cross-functional remote team, and the entire GoHighLevel infrastructure — from CRM pipelines and automation workflows to DNS management and A2P compliance. The technical foundation makes me a better ops lead. The ops mindset makes me a sharper technologist.

I live at the intersection of both. Where most people see a bottleneck, I see a workflow waiting to be designed.
